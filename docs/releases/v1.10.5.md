# NFI Backtest Engine v1.10.5

v1.10.5 restores deterministic Futures free-wallet projection while preserving native
simulation and trade semantics.

## Full-state parity repair

Freqtrade and the native engine reconstruct Futures free balance through economically
equivalent arithmetic paths. Their raw decimal tokens can diverge below one
nano-USDT, including `8022.005733333` versus `8022.005733333333`.

The native observer projection now canonicalizes Futures free balance to one
nano-USDT with half-even rounding. Simulation state and wallet accounting retain
their exact values. The parity comparator still requires byte-for-byte equality over
the projected state; no epsilon or fuzzy comparison was introduced.

Two affected derived state projections were regenerated from their immutable
official state traces to use the same representation. Official raw traces and trade
surfaces remain unchanged.

## Nightly validation

This repair restores exact trade and state parity for all seven fixtures that failed
the nightly full-state shards and preserves the newer release-candidate fixture. The
deduplicated summary failure was downstream of the shard failures, not a separate
defect.

## Upgrade

```text
nfi-bte update
nfi-bte --version
```

The reported version must be `nfi-bte 1.10.5`.
